Academic Buildings Guide

Babbio Center

Houses the School of Business, finance labs, and auditorium halls. Located on the edge of the ridge over the Babbio Parking Lot.

Burchard Hall

This is for mechanical and civil engineering labs. Located at the center right next to Edwin A. Stevens Hall.

Howe Center

This building is for student affairs and also where the Pierce Dining Hall is located. It is a tall building overlooking Castle Point.

Edwin A. Stevens Hall

Building that is specially for Electrical and Computer Engineering, Physics, and home of the Debaun Auditorium. Right next to Burchard Hall.
